# TYTO render briefs

## 01 — TYTO hero

A photorealistic, premium studio product render of **TYTO**, a compact audio device: a microphone that mounts on a camera hot-shoe or a phone rig.

### Product design

- Solid, rounded nautilus/ammonite-shell body; smooth logarithmic spiral coils inward to a small raised spiral eye at the centre; soft, pebble-like volume with generous fillets and no sharp edges.
- The shell tail flares into a short cylindrical mouth with a precision-machined ring, fine diamond knurling, and a recessed circular woven black speaker/mic mesh grille with visible weave.
- A thin, continuous amber-orange LED line is inlaid in a shallow groove, follows the spiral from the centre outward, and ends at a small tick mark on the knurled ring. Soft glow and slight bloom; no strong illumination of the surroundings.
- A subtle parting seam runs around the lower body.
- Dark bronze, finely bead-blasted anodized aluminium with a soft satin sheen on the outer shell; matte black soft-touch inner spiral channel; slightly lighter bronze knurled ring with crisp micro-reflections.

### Render style

- Hero shot, three-quarter view from slightly above; product in the lower third-to-centre of frame; slight rotation so both the spiral and mesh opening are visible.
- Dark warm-grey seamless backdrop, soft gradient, faint realistic contact shadow, subtle floor reflection.
- Large softbox key from upper left; thin strip-light rim from behind right; gentle fill; controlled specular highlight rolling across the curved shell. Amber line is the only colour accent.
- 85 mm macro-style lens, f/5.6, shallow depth of field; sharp focus on the spiral centre and knurled ring; soft falloff toward the back.
- 8K, ray-traced, physically based materials, micro-scratches, fingerprint-free surfaces, accurate reflections, crisp edges, subtle film grain, high dynamic range; Apple / Teenage Engineering / Bang & Olufsen product-photography quality.

### Avoid

Text, logos, extra buttons, cables, hands, plastic look, cartoon look, distorted spiral, uneven knurling, blown-out highlights, oversaturated glow, cluttered background, duplicated parts, warped geometry.
